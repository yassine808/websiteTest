Chapter I — Origins

The world forgot.
The virus did not.

Twenty-six years have passed since Raccoon City was erased from the map. The T-Virus incident became legend, then folklore, then a conspiracy theory whispered on dark web forums. The survivors aged. The agencies crumbled. The world decided it had survived.

It was wrong.

"We didn't destroy Umbrella. We scattered it. And something in the dark has been patiently gathering the pieces."

Deep beneath the ruins of Eastern Europe, a successor organization — known only as REQUIEM — has spent two decades perfecting what Spencer began. Their weapon is not a virus. It is an ending. Airborne. Adaptive. Unstoppable without the singular key that one woman carries unknowingly in her blood.

Her name is Zara Solano. She has never heard of Raccoon City. She is about to become its last chapter.

Chapter II — Systems

What hunts you

SYS — 01

Adaptive Horror Engine

The new A.I. director reads your heartbeat through controller input. The longer you survive, the smarter the creature becomes. There are no scripted scares. Only learning ones.

SYS — 02

Reactive World Mutation

The environments breathe. Fungal networks spread through walls as you explore, sealing paths and opening others. The village that welcomed you will not let you leave.

SYS — 03

Dual-Soul Narrative

Play as both the hunted and the infected. Experience the same catastrophe from opposite sides of the mutation — human instinct vs. viral consciousness.

SYS — 04

Persistent Infection System

Wounds don't just drain health. They spread. A bite left untreated mutates your character over hours of gameplay, altering abilities, dialogue, and available endings.

SYS — 05

Ancient Architecture

A collapsed Soviet facility built atop something far older. Two civilisations of horror interlocked in stone and steel. Map your descent. Know that not all levels lead back up.

SYS — 06

The Resonance

A 3D audio system so precise you can hear an infected creature's breathing pattern change through walls. Silence is information. Silence is survival.

Chapter III — Biohazard Registry

Known strains

Ω-Strain

Class: Engineered / Airborne

The primary weapon of REQUIEM. Recombinant DNA derived from T, G, and Mold. No host rejection. Near-perfect lethality. Theoretical antigen identified in subject S-9.

Vetiver

Class: Fungal / Symbiotic

A self-aware mycological network stretching 40 km beneath the facility. Individual infected act as nerve endings for a singular, ancient intelligence below.

Requiem-X

Class: Unknown / Classified

Existence unconfirmed. Three separate BSAA operatives reported a creature of impossible scale in sub-level 9. All three failed to return from their second deployment.
